## Details

### New
- **Notification center.** A new 🔔 bell with an unread badge opens a list of all your notifications, split into *New* and *Earlier*:
  - friend requests (with an inline **Accept** button), accepted requests and new friends from invite links
  - tags, @mentions, messages in your memories, and likes on your memories and messages
  - several messages or likes on the same memory are grouped into one row ("c and 1 other…")
  - tapping a notification opens the profile or memory; for messages it scrolls to the message and highlights it
  - in-app banners while the app is open, push notifications while it isn't
  - "Read all", pull to refresh and an "All caught up" empty state
- **On this day.** A card and a daily local reminder bring back memories taken on today's date in past years, with a reveal animation. You can set the reminder time in Settings.
- **User profiles.** Open any user's profile from the feed, friends list or a memory. Profiles have shareable links (`/u/<username>`).
- **Save photo.** A new options sheet on the draft photo saves it to your photo library.
- **Facebook sign-in** on the sign-in and sign-up screens.

### Improved
- **Map clusters.** Tapping a cluster opens a new sheet that groups its memories by day, with a quick peek at each one. It replaces the old cluster and place sheets.
- **Settings.** The profile menu has been redesigned. Settings › Notifications now has a switch for each type (Friend requests, Tags, Messages, Likes, On this day). Likes are off by default.
- **Navigation.** You can now swipe between tabs, and the floating tab bar has been updated.
- Camera zoom chips, avatars and tagged-friend lines have been polished.

### Fixed
- Several small bugs in the camera, drafts, avatar upload and pin creation flows.
